## ⚔️ Hollow Chamber

A 2D Hades-style arena roguelite. Clear three chambers of the underworld, choose a boon after every wave, and fight your way back to the surface.

No build step — open `index.html` directly, or serve the folder with any static server:

```bash
python3 -m http.server 8000
```

### Choose your weapon

| Weapon | Attack | Special |
| --- | --- | --- |
| **Stygian Blade** | Three-hit combo; the finisher carves wider and hits harder | **Whirlwind** — spin through everything nearby |
| **Aetos Spear** | Long piercing thrust that skewers a line of foes | **Eagle's Flight** — throw and recall the spear |
| **Heart-Seeker Bow** | Rapid arrows that punish from afar | **Five Fates** — loose a fan of five arrows |

### Controls

- **Desktop:** `WASD` move, mouse aim, **LMB** attack, **RMB** special, `Space` dash, `Esc` pause.
- **Mobile:** virtual joystick to move, on-screen **Attack / Special / Dash** buttons. Attacks auto-aim at the nearest foe. Layout respects safe areas in portrait and landscape.
- Weapon cards can be picked with `1` / `2` / `3` or a click/tap; `Enter` starts the run.

Progress, boons, and audio settings live only in the browser. Mute persists across reloads.
